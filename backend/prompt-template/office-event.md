Give special attention to the most important event moments, such as speeches, awards, announcements, team-building activities, or performances, depending on what is available in the photos.

The final pages should create a warm closing feeling: pride, teamwork, gratitude, celebration, and shared memory.

- Activities / performances / entertainment: 4–6 pages
- Food, networking, and informal moments: 3–4 pages
- Group portraits and team spirit: 3–4 pages
- Celebration and closing: 2–3 pages

This allocation can be adjusted depending on the event type and available photos, but the final plan should feel balanced, purposeful, and story-driven.
